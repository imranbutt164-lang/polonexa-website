Baby diapers wholesale supplier to Nigeria

Polonexa Trade Co supplies baby diapers by the container to importers, wholesalers and distributors in Nigeria and across Africa. We offer a premium range and a value range in every size from newborn to XX-large.

Diaper ranges and sizes

RangeSizesBest for
Premium rangeNewborn, Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large, XX-LargeSupermarkets, pharmacies and modern trade
Value rangeNewborn, Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large, XX-LargeOpen markets, wholesale and price-led retail

Diapers are packed in retail packs inside export cases. Both ranges and all sizes can be mixed in one container, so you can match your order to what sells in your market. The minimum order is one 20ft container.

Diaper prices in Nigeria: how we quote

Shelf prices for diapers in Nigeria move with the naira, freight and brand. We quote a firm price per case, with the price per pack and per diaper shown so you can plan your retail margin. Your landed cost depends on:

  • The range and size mix in the container.
  • Ocean freight to Lagos (Apapa or Tin Can) or your chosen port.
  • Nigerian duty, levies and the exchange rate when you open Form M.
  • Clearing and inland delivery to your warehouse.
Get a quotation. Tell us the sizes, quantities and destination port, and we will send prices per case, per pack and per diaper. Request a quote.

Documents, inspection and payment

Every shipment comes with a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin, plus the product certificates your market needs, and we confirm SONCAP and any other Nigerian registration requirements with you before shipment. Independent inspection by SGS, Intertek or an equivalent agency is available at the load port. Payment is by T/T advance with the balance against shipping documents, or by irrevocable L/C at sight from a prime bank.
