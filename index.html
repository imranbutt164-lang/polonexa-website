About us

Trade built around demand, quality and execution.

We connect producers, mills and processors in Asia and Europe with buyers in Africa, and we handle the parts of a shipment that usually go wrong: specification, inspection, documents and logistics.

Every enquiry is checked against supplier availability, the buyer's specification, market conditions and the import rules of the destination country before we commit. What we quote is what we ship.

How we trade

From enquiry to shipment

Right product, right specification, right buyer, right logistics.

  1. EnquiryProduct, grade, quantity, packing and destination port.
  2. SpecificationGrade, labelling and shelf life aligned with qualified suppliers.
  3. QuotationFormal offer with Incoterms, validity, payment and shipment terms.
  4. ConfirmationCountersigned Proforma Invoice and advance or L/C.
  5. DocumentsInspection, certificates and destination paperwork prepared.
  6. ShipmentContainer loading, Bill of Lading and document release.
