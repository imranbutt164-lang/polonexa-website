Documents, inspection and payment

Every shipment comes with a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin, plus the product certificates your market needs, including health certificates. Independent inspection by SGS, Intertek or an equivalent agency is available at the load port. Payment is by T/T advance with the balance against shipping documents, or by irrevocable L/C at sight from a prime bank.

Frequently asked questions

Which pasta products do you supply?

Spaghetti in 400 g packs and macaroni in 500 g packs, in a choice of shapes.

Can you ship pasta to Nigeria?

No. Pasta, spaghetti and noodles are on Nigeria's import prohibition list, so we do not ship pasta there.

Which countries do you supply pasta to?

The GCC states and East African markets such as Kenya and Tanzania.

What is the minimum order for pasta?

One 20ft container. Pasta can be combined with other products to fill it.
