Green and black table olives supplier

Polonexa Trade Co exports Iranian green and black table olives to importers, wholesalers, retailers and food-service buyers in the GCC and Africa. Olives are available whole, pitted or sliced, in brine.

Olive types we supply

ProductCutsBest for
Green olivesWhole, pitted, slicedRetail jars, salads, mezze and catering
Black olivesWhole, pitted, slicedPizza, sandwiches, bakeries and food service

Pack sizes run from retail jars and tins to bulk food-service packs. We confirm the pack, drained weight and calibre for each order. Olives can share a container with other Polonexa lines.

Markets we serve

We supply olives to importers and distributors in Oman, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Qatar and Kuwait, and to buyers in East and West Africa. Our Muscat and Manama offices handle GCC orders directly.

Documents, inspection and payment

Every shipment comes with a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin, plus the product certificates your market needs, including health and analysis certificates. Independent inspection by SGS, Intertek or an equivalent agency is available at the load port. Payment is by T/T advance with the balance against shipping documents, or by irrevocable L/C at sight from a prime bank.

Frequently asked questions

Where are your olives from?

Our table olives are sourced from Iran.

Do you supply green and black olives?

Yes. Both are available whole, pitted or sliced, in brine.

Which countries do you ship olives to?

Mainly the GCC: Oman, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Qatar and Kuwait. We also ship to buyers in Africa.

Can I order olives with other products?

Yes. Olives can be combined with rice, coffee, powder drinks and other Polonexa lines in one container.
